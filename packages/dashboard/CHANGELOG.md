# @adminigloo/dashboard

## 0.1.0

New package: the staff dashboard. A card for each part of the app (what needs you, a few counts, a short list, the way to the full page), arranged by each person and saved for them on the server (`dashboard_layouts`), offered only to staff who may open the page behind it. Ready-made cards for feedback, website visits, calls, search check, AI mentions, the assistant, companies, people, the shop, company plans, errors and the audit log.
