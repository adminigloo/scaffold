# @adminigloo/digest

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- ad0405a: First release: each person's own email choices and a daily digest in their own time zone. `defineEmailKinds({ digests, alerts })` declares what a person can choose — digests built from SECTIONS (a server function of the person and the day returning `{ title, text, html }` or null; a day where every section is null sends nothing; `html` must come from the escaping `html` tag) and alert kinds — every one OFF until the person says yes. `createDigests({ db, tables, kinds, people, send, … })`: preferences (zone from the browser at sign-up, else the site's; a local delivery hour, 7 by default), `run()` — one email per person per kind per local day, from their hour until the next day's, claimed in a ledger (`digest_sends`, a unique index) before anything is built, so a replay, a second instance or a retry sends nobody two; paced (Resend's 2 a second), budgeted (no send starts unless it and a margin fit), the longest-waiting first, "skipped" (never "failed", never retried) with no email provider, retries with the same idempotency key only when `idempotentSends` — `preview()` (staff: any person, any day, built and never sent), `sendAlert()` (only to people who chose the kind, at most once per `dedupeKey`), `applySignUpChoice()`, `forgetPerson()`. Every email carries RFC 8058's one-click `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and a footer link to an unsubscribe page; the link is the person and the kind under an HMAC of the site's secret, so it works without signing in, stops only that email for only that person, and another person's link — or a changed one — is refused. `createDigestHandlers` serves the one-click POST and the page's form (no sign-in; a GET never unsubscribes), the signed-in person's preferences (GET, PUT from the same site only) and the staff preview at `/v1/preview`. DST-safe clock from `Intl` alone (`deliveryWindow`, `zonedInstant`, `localDay`). Tables under a prefix with `defineDigestTables({ prefix })`. `./ui`: `<EmailPreferences>` and `<SignUpEmailChoice>` (unticked; the browser's zone and the boxes ticked in a short-lived cookie the first page applies once).
