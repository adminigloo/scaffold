An AI assistant for each company (create-app 0.29.0, Tenancy 2b-3).

- **Companies mode** (`--tenant-noun …` with `--assistant`): an "Ask the assistant" button on every page of a company's area (/app/…, `app/(site)/app/layout.tsx` over the generated `src/components/AssistantLauncherSlot.tsx`), over `/api/app/{id}/assistant/…`; and **/app/assistant**, the owner's settings — the business profile, answers to common questions, house rules, words, and this month's AI use against the allowance. The company's keys (`assistant.use`, `assistant.settings.manage`) are in the tenant catalog; /app/assistant is in the company nav.
- **Starter tools wired to the installed features**, through each package's own read and permission (`src/server/assistant-tools.ts`): "What's on the schedule tomorrow?" (scheduling), "Which invoices are overdue?" (invoicing), the price book (estimator) — reads only, figures worked out in code — and one offered change: remember an answer as an FAQ, saved only on "Yes, do it".
- **Spend**: with `--ai`, Claude Haiku 4.5 (`ASSISTANT_MODEL` to change it), each company's month (`ASSISTANT_MONTHLY_MESSAGES` 300 / `ASSISTANT_MONTHLY_CENTS` 300 in src/server/assistant.ts) and the site's day (`AI_SITE_DAILY_CENTS` 500 in src/server/ai.ts), checked before every question and failing closed, every question metered in ai_usage. A project that sells plans gets "AI assistant messages a month" on each plan (src/plans.ts, enforced through `assistantMessageLimit` in src/server/plan-gate.ts). Without `--ai` the settings work and the chat says it isn't switched on.
- **This site's own assistant** stays at /admin/assistant (now on `staff.assistant.manage`), with its chat at /admin/assistant/chat over `/api/admin/assistant` (`staff.assistant.use`, with `--ai`). A single business keeps one assistant: the site's, with the feature tools on their staff keys.
- **The isolation proof** drives `ASSISTANT_ADMIN_ROUTES` (B with A's conversation, offered-change, rule, word and answer ids gets "not found"; A's rows are unchanged; each role is refused what it doesn't hold) with a scripted model, and checks B's assistant tools never return A's rows.
- **`--ai`** now calls Claude Haiku 4.5 by default, caps each answer at 2,000 tokens, records usage through `@adminigloo/ai/server`, and /api/ai/chat also checks the company's month (`AI_CHAT_MONTHLY_CENTS`) and the site's day.
- After an independent review: each question's place is held before the model is called (`reserveAiAllowance`) and settled with its real cost, a stopped answer included; a per-person (10 a minute) and per-company (30 a minute) rate limit on the shared limiter; every company has a cents safety cap — 7¢ × its questions (the worst case a question can cost at Haiku prices, worked out in a comment), $50 for unlimited — and at most a quarter of the site's day (`ASSISTANT_DAILY_SHARE_CENTS`); the site owner is told in /admin/errors when the site's day is spent, and the estimator's photo reading checks the same ceiling; the saved-answer card shows question and answer and can be edited; the schedule and invoice tools aren't "authoritative" (customers' words); overdue invoices past 200 say "at least 200"; the price-book starter names one of the company's own products. A companies project whose only field feature is invoicing passes its isolation proof again (the mixed pass is required only where a route names two rows).
- Versions: assistant 0.9.0, assistant-widget 0.3.0, ai 0.2.0.
