Consumer fit (create-app 0.39). **`create-app upgrade --audience consumers`** and **`upgrade --set <answer>=<value>`** (tenantNoun, audience, businessModel/model, adminShell/admin, or a feature on or off, repeatable) switch a project's answers while upgrading — re-planned with the new answers by the same three-way as a version upgrade: new files written, untouched files rewritten, untouched retired files deleted, `<file>.new` beside every changed one; on an untouched project the result is byte for byte a fresh generation with the new answers (but src/plans.ts, which a switch never rewrites — the new sample goes beside it as src/plans.ts.new, since its tiers are the ones people pay for), and a project already at this version gets only the switch. **Nothing is lost without being asked:** an upgrade or a switch that would DROP tables (a feature or a model switched off, a package this version no longer uses — read off the two src/db/schema.ts files), change what an account is (companies or one person each) or stop a plan check is refused before it writes anything, with every loss listed, until `--allow-data-loss` is given — and then the dropped tables are printed by name, `pnpm db:migrate` is never a routine step (read the migration first), a kept page is said to still be served, and a companies project asked for `--audience consumers` is told why not without being pointed at a tenantNoun switch. Every "did you change this file?" comparison (upgrade and add) ignores line endings, so a CRLF file an older create-app wrote and `git checkout` turned into LF is still untouched. **Plans are not a shop:** `--model subscription` installs stripe and billing only — no /products, /checkout, /account/orders, admin Products, licence keys, seed-shop, catalog or commerce packages, or their tables (the stripe overlay is the plans' half; the shop is the new `shop`, `account-shop` and `shop-admin` overlays, for one-time and both). **Consumers are one person:** no /settings/team, /members, /invite pages or invitation email; the seat limit of one; no role, the owner included, holds the invite, remove, change-role or hand-over keys (`HELD_BY_NOBODY`, sealed — and `pnpm db:seed` takes them back from a role that held them, with permissions 0.4); each person is a member of their own account only (src/server/membership.ts), so a membership left from the --audience business days is refused everywhere — `requireEntitlement` included — and `pnpm db:seed` suspends it, naming it (src/permissions/shared-accounts.ts); access, billing and the account pages read the person's OWN account (src/server/tenant.ts); a sender for the site's own emails (src/server/mail.ts) with --email. **Crawlable pages:** the group-wide app/(site)/loading.tsx is gone (it turned redirects and not-founds into 200s and hid every page behind "Loading"); the account pages keep a loading boundary below their heading; a generated test (`pnpm build && pnpm test:pages`) checks every sitemap page answers a real 200 with its h1 in the first HTML, a missing page 404 and a signed-in page a real 307. **Founder lockout:** the no-access page names `pnpm admin:grant <your email>` (the GitHub workflow only for a project that deploys from GitHub), its wording chosen by `isLocalRun`. The first administrator is BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_EMAIL whenever it is set, on any machine; with it unset, the first sign-in only on the dev server — never on a production build, whose localhost app URL a self-hosted server shares (src/server/__tests__/first-administrator.test.ts). **One plan presentation** (`PlanCard`, from billing's `planColumns`) on /pricing, the landing page's plans and /account/billing: `free:` and `onSale` in src/plans.ts (checkout refuses anything not on sale, the plans aren't published to Stripe, and a refusal names only plans on sale), /account and /account/billing reading what you're on — the tier marked yours, Free, the features listed — from the plan check's own standing (never the row's status: ended free months and unfinished checkouts are not a plan), no "No …" lines, "Everything in Free, plus:", a Free column, whole-dollar prices, yearly first, "Most popular" only where the record sets it, "your account" wording for people, the operator checklist for staff only, and the pricing page's buttons go to the plan chooser (`?plan=`) through sign-up. **A consumer dashboard** at /admin: subscribers by plan and line, monthly vs yearly, MRR and ARR at what each subscription is actually charged, new (first paid — never a trial) and cancelled (never a conversion) this month, failed payments, free to paid, free months only until they end (src/server/subscriber-numbers.ts, tested there and on the mirror's own rows), and the shop's tiles only with a shop. **Conversions** with --analytics: a NEW account's sign-up (via /welcome) and its first payment (back from Stripe's checkout, /account/billing/return, judged on Stripe's own Checkout Session — paid, more than nothing, so never a free trial) mark the visit converted, once per person (analytics' `once`). Needs @adminigloo/billing 0.6, @adminigloo/env 0.4, @adminigloo/permissions 0.4, @adminigloo/stripe 0.7 and @adminigloo/analytics 0.5.
