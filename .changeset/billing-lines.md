Product lines and personal plans (billing 0.5). `definePlans({ lines: { weather: { name, features, tiers }, sports: { … } } })` declares several things sold on their own, each with its own tiers and vocabulary (checked per line at compile time; tier keys unique across lines); a catalog written without `lines` is one implicit line (`DEFAULT_PLAN_LINE`, "main"), so every existing record reads the same. Tiers and rows carry `line`; the catalog has `lines` and `line(key)`. `subscriptions` gains `line` (default "main") and `stripe_item_id`, and its unique indexes are per line: at most one live row per (tenant, line) (`subscriptions_tenant_line_live_idx`) and one per (Stripe subscription, line). `planGrantDiff(current, tier, { refs: planLineRefs(catalog, line) })` touches only that line's plan rows, so changing or cancelling one line never revokes another's. `loadCompanyPlan(db, { …, plans })` works out a standing per line (`lines`, with each line's newest subscription) and the best of them as `standing` (`combineStandings`); `liveStripeLines` lists every live Stripe-billed line, and `lineOfStoredRow` places a row from before lines by its tier.
