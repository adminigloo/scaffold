Each person's own emails, and SEO by default (create-app 0.40, from the review of the founder's Almanac). **Each person's own emails** (`--audience consumers --email`, @adminigloo/digest 0.1): every person chooses their own emails at **/account/email** (a new Email tab; the whole account area in a project that sells nothing) — each digest and alert kind in the project's own **src/digests.ts**, every one OFF until they tick it — and the local hour their digests come in their own zone (7 AM; the zone from their browser at sign-up, else `SITE_TIME_ZONE`). The sign-up page offers each digest beside Clerk's form, UNTICKED (`<SignUpEmailSlot />`, nothing in any other project), and **/welcome** keeps what was ticked and the browser's zone, once. A digest is SECTIONS the project registers — each a server function `(person, day) => { title, text, html } | null`, html from the escaping `html` tag; with plans, a real sample section ships (what needs the person on their own account, in the plan banner's words, null on every other day), and a digest with no section is offered nowhere. **/api/cron/digest** sends one email per person per local day at or after their hour — claimed in a ledger before anything is built, so a replay sends nobody anything; ends by 50 seconds and reports who is still waiting; "skipped", never "failed" or reported, with no email provider; daily at 15:00 UTC in vercel.json (hourly on Pro, DEPLOYMENT.md). Every email carries RFC 8058's one-click `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and a signed link to **/unsubscribe** (no sign-in, asks first, undo; stops only that email; another person's link is refused); `digests.sendAlert(…)` emails only the people who chose an alert kind, once per key; staff with the new "Preview people's emails" key (admin, cs_lead) see anybody's digest for any day at **/api/email/v1/preview**, never sent. src/server/mail.ts sends `headers` and an `idempotencyKey` (email 0.5) and answers `{ status, error, retryable }` on a failure. Proved in every such project by src/server/__tests__/digest.test.ts (two people in two zones, the replay, no provider, unsubscribe by the header and by the link, a forged link, the preview staff-only). `EMAIL_LINK_SECRET` (optional; else a key made from CLERK_SECRET_KEY). **SEO by default**, in every project: a tab icon, home-screen icons and **/manifest.webmanifest** drawn from the name and src/brand.ts, the site's share card at a FIXED address (**/og.png**; Next 16 hashes a root opengraph-image), ONE public-page list (**src/site-pages.ts**) for the sitemap, **/llms.txt** and the new **/llms-full.txt**, with **`dynamicPages()`** (src/dynamic-pages.ts) adding database-backed pages to all three, which are cached for an hour instead of frozen at build; **proxy.ts** is generated and sends **X-Robots-Tag** on every private route (sign-in, sign-up, /account, /admin, /api, /setup, /welcome, /unsubscribe) from **src/seo-paths.ts**, the list robots.txt reads, and with --analytics logs every AI crawler's read of any page after the answer from a module loaded only then (a person's request costs one user-agent test; app/llms.txt no longer logs its own); every generated public page exports `metadataFor({ path, title, description })` (src/seo.ts; @adminigloo/seo's `pageMetadata` with --seo).
