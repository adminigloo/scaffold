First release: spendable balances. `wallet_balances` (one row per holder per unit, never below zero, a CHECK) and an append-only `wallet_ledger` (`grant`, `spend`, `refund`, `adjust`) whose unique `(holder_id, unit, reason, ref)` index makes every movement idempotent: a redelivered webhook, a double click, or the webhook and the page both settling one payment move the balance once. `creditWallet`, `spendWallet` (one conditional UPDATE, refusing with `InsufficientCreditsError` and the balance held), `refundWallet` (takes back what's left, never below zero, recorded once even when it takes nothing), `adjustWallet` (staff: a note and an actor required, refuses to go below zero), and the reads `walletBalance`, `balancesOf`, `listWallets`, `listWalletLedger`. Each write is ONE statement, so it is atomic alone or inside a caller's transaction and needs no interactive transaction. Several kinds of credit side by side, each a unit slug (`crowns`, `ai-credits`). `walletPermissions`: `credits.balances.view` and `credits.balances.adjust` (staff). `formatCredits` and the checks are browser-safe from the root entry; `sql/wallet-0.1.0.sql`.
