History and housekeeping. **Delete a company** at /admin/companies/{id} (staff with "Delete companies for good", admin by default): src/server/forget-company.ts calls every installed package's `forgetTenant` on tenancy's deletion transaction, children first, deletes the company's files once it commits, and names a plan the company is still paying for; tenancy keeps one audit row. The proof (src/server/__tests__/forget-company.test.ts) plants a row for two companies in every table with a company column — read from the database, so a new table can't be missed — deletes one through the real service and checks that nothing of it is left (outside `COMPANY_ROWS_KEPT`, each with its reason) and nothing of the other is gone. The per-company coverage test requires every per-company package to say how its rows are forgotten. A Stripe event for a deleted company (a forced delete leaves the subscription live at Stripe) writes nothing back: src/server/subscription.ts acknowledges it (`tenantGone`) and the webhook logs a warning to cancel it there; the proof replays one. **Past records with their real dates** reach the generated projects through the packages' staff routes (estimator `occurredAt`, invoicing `issuedAt`/`paidAt`, scheduling `bookedAt`/`occurredAt`), behind their own "Enter past records" keys (a single business maps them to staff.estimates.settings, staff.invoices.manage and staff.schedule.settings) and `analytics.importVisits`. **The store's sales by hand** (`POST …/store/v1/admin/sales`) are told to the store's `onOrder`, so they reach the customer's timeline and the receipt goes the same way.
