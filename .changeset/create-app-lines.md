Billing 0.5: product lines and personal plans. **`--audience consumers`** (with `--tenant-noun none`; asked when there are no companies, `business` by default, so every existing project is unchanged): the people who sign up are the customers. With `--model subscription` or `both`, each person's own workspace pays for its plans — src/server/plan-gate.ts's personal variant (a free account stays open; an optional Stripe-side trial with BILLING_TRIAL_DAYS), a banner about the person's own plans, two sample product lines in src/plans.ts with no team members, the proof src/server/__tests__/personal-plans.test.ts — and in every consumers project nobody can share an account (a seat limit of one; the team page says the account is just yours, and no nav links to it). The founder sees who pays for which line and tier, and gives free months, at **/admin/billing/subscribers**. Wherever plans are sold, **src/server/access.ts** answers one question for any paid feature — `requireEntitlement(feature)` (tRPC), `withEntitlement(feature, handler)` (route handlers), `pageEntitlement` and `<Entitled feature>` (pages) — with the sentence to show when the plan doesn't include it. **Product lines** (`definePlans({ lines })`) stack on ONE Stripe subscription: `/account/billing` shows a card per line, `billing.previewAddLine` / `billing.addLine` add a line to the plan already paid (server-priced, prorated, idempotent, refused when it's already on it at Stripe — read item by item the way the mirror reads them — and a line added back after a drop is a real new add, mirrored from a fresh read), a tier change swaps the line's own item, cancelling one of several lines removes its item with the unused time kept as credit on the account, free months are refused only on a line already paid by card, and every Stripe-sourced write mirrors the whole subscription one line per item (src/server/subscription.ts's `mirrorStripeSubscription`: an item resolves by its price, its Price's own stamp, its metadata, or our own row for it), ending a line whose item is gone — never one whose item Stripe still bills. A shop's recurring product now ends what it granted when its subscription ends; in a person's own workspace plan and shop checkouts share one Stripe customer (a shop subscription never counts as a plan), and in a company a member's own purchases stay on their own customer. The money paths are proved in every project that takes money (src/server/__tests__/product-lines.test.ts, product-grants.test.ts).
