A store of its own for every business (getting paid, part 2). A business's products are catalog rows under its own tenant id (one default variant: price in cents, currency, an optional stock count; "*" stays the site's own plans and products). `@adminigloo/commerce/store` is the server half: `createStoreProduct`/`updateStoreProduct`/`removeStoreProduct` (a plan's product limit checked under the business's lock), `startStoreCheckout` (every line priced from the business's own product rows under their row locks — the cart names products and quantities only; stock HELD on catalog 0.3's `product_variants.held` under the variants' locks in id order — shelf minus held is what can be sold, so carts never overwrite the owner's count and an owner's edit is refused in words if sales moved it meanwhile; the same unchanged cart gets its page back before any stock check and its own hold counts as its own; holds last 30 minutes and are given back at 31; a visitor may have 3 open checkouts and open 5 an hour (callers with no address share one), and an order at most `maxPerOrder` (default 5) of a product; the hold is reserved and committed before Stripe is called, and given back in its own transaction if Stripe fails; shipping ("I ship it" with a flat price and the owner's countries, or pickup/digital only) is a server line in the exact amount and the address is kept on the order), `handleStoreEvent` for the webhook (an order only for a checkout of the business the account belongs to, in exactly its amount and currency, once — keyed on the Checkout Session; bank payments held then cleared or released; refunds in part or in full onto `orders.refunded_minor`, in the order's currency only; disputes flagged, oversold kept apart in `orders.oversold_at`; the order's emails sent once each and retried until they go), holds given back exactly once when a page expires, fails, is replaced or a product in it changes price (THE EDIT RULE) or the owner presses "Release held items", store settings in `store_settings`, and "Needs your attention" in `store_alerts`. `createStoreAdminHandlers` and `createStorePublicHandlers` serve `STORE_ADMIN_ROUTES` and `STORE_PUBLIC_ROUTES` (in the root, with `storePermissions` and `storeCompanyPermissions`: `store.view`, `store.manage`); `storeOrderEmails` sends "{business} via {app}" with replies to the owner. `@adminigloo/commerce/widget` has the screens: `<StoreAdmin>`, `<StoreAttention>`, `<Storefront>` (the cart) and `<StoreThanks>`. Run `sql/upgrade-0.1-to-0.2.sql` before deploying: it adds `orders.refunded_minor`, the order flags and email marks, `store_checkouts`, `store_alerts` and `store_settings`, and keeps every existing order. Product photos are a storage purpose (`STORE_PHOTO_PURPOSE`): public, counted in the business's allowance. The screens take their colours from the app's theme tokens, light and dark.
