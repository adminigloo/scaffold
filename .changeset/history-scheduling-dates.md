Past records with their real dates (staff and server paths only; no public route takes a date): `bookVisit(…, { bookedAt })` and `POST v1/admin/visits` with `bookedAt` save a visit as booked that day. History only when the visit itself is over: then it isn't held to today's hours and nobody is told; a visit still to come is checked like any booking (a taken slot, outside hours) and the customer told as usual, so a booked-on date never puts a second visit in a slot; `setBookingStatus(…, { occurredAt })` and `POST v1/admin/visits/{id}/status` with `occurredAt` record it done, cancelled or started that day (done, started or no-show never before the visit starts; on a visit still to come the move is checked and told as usual, and putting a cancelled one back re-claims its slot). Their events carry the real day and `enteredLater: true`; `schedulingAlertsFor` raises no alert for them. A date in the future, before 2000 or before the booking is refused (400 `invalid`, naming the field). A date alone ("2025-06-14") is that day where the company is (its time zone; noon there, never moved before the record it belongs to or past the moment it was typed in), and "today" is today there — a morning entry in Tokyo is not "in the future"; a full timestamp is the instant it names. A past date on the staff routes needs its own key, `schedule.history` ("Enter past records", owner and admin by default); without it, 403 in plain words. The visit's snapshot carries `enteredLater` (when, and by whom). New nullable `scheduling_bookings.entered_at`/`entered_by`.

**Upgrading from 0.6:** two nullable columns (`pnpm db:generate`, or the README's "Upgrading from 0.6").
