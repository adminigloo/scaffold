No "Something went wrong" where the cause is knowable. An answer that isn't the package's own (a platform's error page, a proxy, a timeout) is read by its status — signed out (401), no permission (403), not here any more (404), too big (413), too many tries (429), the site not answering (502, 503) or too slow (504) — instead of "Unexpected answer from the server (502)." or the generic line; the generic sentence is left for a truly unknown failure. A dropped connection on the customer's invoice page says so, and "Couldn't reach the server." tells the person to check their internet connection.
