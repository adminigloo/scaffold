Getting paid, part 2: `--store` (and `create-app add store`). With companies, each company's products and orders at /app/store, its store at /t/{web address}/store with the cart and the thank-you page, paid into its own connected Stripe account (direct charges, PLATFORM_FEE_BPS) and recorded by the Connect webhook for the company that owns the account; store payments that need the owner on /app/payments beside invoices'. One business: /admin/store and /store on its own Stripe keys, through its payments webhook (/api/webhooks/stripe-invoices). Order emails "{company} via {site}" reply to the owner. Plans sell "products in your store" (Starter 20, Pro 200). The isolation proof drives the store's routes; `store.view` and `store.manage` are company keys (staff keys for one business). No new Stripe webhook events. The site's own checkout now refuses any product that isn't the site's own.

The store's settings ("How customers get it": I ship it — a flat shipping price and the countries — or pickup/digital only; prices include any sales tax) are on /app/store and /admin/store; shipped orders show the address. Product photos are the company's own files, counted in its storage allowance, 30 uploads an hour. A trial company sends at most 20 store receipts a day. The admin dashboard counts only this site's own orders and products. Every public throttle (store, booking, invoicing, estimator, scheduling) now gives a caller with no forwarded address one shared "unknown" budget instead of none.
